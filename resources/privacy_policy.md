Your privacy is important to us. The [{{app}} Privacy Policy]({{privacyPolicyURL}}) covers how we collect, use, disclose, transfer, and store your personal information across every {{app}} service, {{bot}} included.

Here’s what {{bot}} itself does with your data.

## 📥 What {{bot}} stores

Polls, and nothing else. {{bot}} keeps each poll’s options and vote counts, plus your user ID and display name next to the option you picked, so you can’t vote twice and can still change your mind. It’s all deleted when the poll closes.

## 🚫 What {{bot}} doesn’t store

{{bot}} never stores your messages, your message history, or member lists. Search terms go to the {{app}} API to fetch your results, then they’re gone.

## ⚙️ Your choices

Admins can limit {{bot}} to specific channels with Discord’s own permissions. A search only reads your reply while your results are on screen, and it times out after 30 seconds if you ignore it. Kick {{bot}} and everything stops.

## 💬 Questions

Reach out to an administrator on the [{{app}} Discord]({{supportServerURL}}).
